**What this means for README.md:40:** with no phone channel and no Finnhub key, every outbound call
(N1–N14) is a data source's own request. Those requests still say something about you: Yahoo receives
every watchlist ticker (N1, N10) and every ticker you open (N7–N9), and SEC receives your contact email
(N12–N13). N17 and N18 are not data sources. Once phone alerts are set up, the alert text goes to
ntfy.sh or Telegram, and on public ntfy.sh anyone who knows the topic can read it. The docs say so in
setup.md and getting-started.md, but README.md:40 doesn't.
